Mac & Cheese

Notes

This is a large recipe. It fills my 4.8qt Anchor Hocking Rectangular to the brim, so be prepared and make adjustments for more reasonable sizes. I highly reccomend adding ham and browned or caramelized onions. If adding onions, consider adding some garlic to the pan as you cook them down. Mix-ins should be added to the sauce at the very end, if you choose to. Some people also like to add a bit of sour cream to their mac. I don't, but would guess that it can be added just before the cheese is.

Ingredients

  • Heavy Cream or Evaporated Milk
  • 1.5 Pounds (3 eight ouce Blocks) Cheese, Shredded. I reccomend a combination of smoked gouda, cheddar, and colby jack
  • 2 Pounds Elbows or Shells
  • Enough Butter (1/2 to full stick)
  • 1 Egg, can use 2 to get a firmer result, or replace entirely with a roux
  • Optional: Velveeta, American Cheese, or Emulsifier
  • Suggested Seasonings: Salt, White Pepper, Smoked Paprika, Ground Mustard Seed or Yellow Mustard, a dash of hot sauce (Tabasco reccomended for it's smokey flavor).

Instructions

  1. Put the heavy cream in a saucepan over low-medium heat. Bring to a very low simmer. Slowly add the cheese. The sauce should be very thick but still liquid when warm. You are making an emulsion, so temperature control is important. Velveeta and American Cheese have an emulsifier in them, so using them or an emulsifier makes it much more forgiving to not break your sauce. If the sauce becomes too thick, add some more cream. It should run, but stick to the back of your spoon. Add seasonings and egg (be VERY careful with temperature when addding the egg).
  2. Coat a baking dish/casserole pan and add cooked noodles. Cut butter into slices and lay on top. Pour cheese sauce over butter to melt it and spread among noodles. Carefully stir to distribute the sauce and any add-ins.
  3. Bake at 350 for 30-45 min, or until top is slightly browned and sauce is bubbling around the edges.
