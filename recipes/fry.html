Pan Fried Protein

Notes

Pan fried, not sautéed. Breaded and fried in plenty of oil. The breading will absorb plenty of oil as you fry, so be ready to add more. This recipe works well with fish fillets, which my mother at one point told me she was never successful with, so this is kinda my victory lap on that. Corn starch can make an acceptable substitue for flour is you're cooking gluten free. If you're careful with the oil amount as you finish, you can use the leftovers from the breading to make a delicious marsala sauce.

Ingredients

  • Protein (Fish, Chicken Thigs, Pork Chops, etc.)
  • Egg
  • Milk or Water
  • Flour
  • Seasonings
  • Bread Crumbs
  • Neutral Oil (I would choose something cheap, and avoid olive oil because of it's lower smoke point)

Instructions

  1. Combine egg and milk/water. Should be a consistency similar to that for french toast. ~50/50 ratio
  2. In separrate dish, combine flour and seasonings, don't forget the salt.
  3. Coat protein in flour mixture, then egg mixture, then breadcrumbs.
  4. Put into medium-hot pan, with pre-heated oil. Fry until golden brown, flip carefully. Don't be afraid to add more oil.
